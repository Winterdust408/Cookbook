Why yes I am using version control software to write a cookbook. In part bc I think it's funny to make updates to recipes in the language of bug reports, in the future I hope to use this to show the evolution of recipes as I refine them. I also wanted a convenient place to show recipes to people I might be cooking with.

Note this is a work in progress! Some of these are directly copied from other sources, I try to make it clear when that's the case.

These are mostly dishes I've made at least ten times.

Ones marked with an asterisk* are the best as rated by other people telling me (or the person I got it from) they liked it.
